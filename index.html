FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Good questions deserve clear answers.

These are the basics. Specific requirements can vary by location, class and admission cycle.

Can I enquire for any grade from Nursery to Class XII?

Yes. This page accepts enquiries across those grades. It does not imply that places are currently available in every grade at every campus.

Does submitting an enquiry guarantee admission?

No. An enquiry is not a formal application, acceptance or confirmed seat. The school determines admissions in accordance with its policies.

Can I schedule a campus visit?

Submit an admission callback enquiry, then ask the school counsellor about available campus-visit appointments. Visits must be confirmed directly by the school.

Where can I confirm fees, eligibility and age criteria?

Contact the selected campus or consult its latest official admission information. Fees and eligibility may differ by grade and academic session.
